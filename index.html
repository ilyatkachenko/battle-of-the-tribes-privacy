1. Introduction

Welcome to Battle of the Tribes ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile game Battle of the Tribes (the "Game").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, installing, or playing the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Game, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Identifiers: Device ID, Android Advertising ID (AAID), or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings
  • Network Information: IP address, connection type (WiFi/mobile data)
  • Gameplay Data: Game progress, scores, match history, wins/losses, player ratings
  • Technical Data: Crash logs, performance data, error reports

2.2 Information You Provide

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Player Name: The username you choose for your profile
  • Support Requests: Information you provide when contacting us for support

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Purpose Data Used
Provide multiplayer matchmaking Device ID, Player rating, Network data
Save and sync game progress Gameplay data, Device ID
Display advertisements Device ID, Advertising ID, IP address
Improve game performance Crash logs, Technical data
Prevent cheating and fraud Device ID, Gameplay data
Analytics and optimization All collected data (anonymized)

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information about you. These services have their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to show personalized or non-personalized ads.

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Unity Services (Game Infrastructure)

We use Unity Gaming Services for authentication, cloud saves, matchmaking, and game server hosting. Unity may collect device information and gameplay data.

Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Advertising

Our Game displays advertisements to support free gameplay. We work with Google AdMob to show you ads. You may see:

  • Personalized Ads: Based on your interests and activity (if you consent)
  • Non-Personalized Ads: Generic ads not based on your personal data

Your Advertising Choices

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA): We will ask for your consent before showing personalized advertisements. You can change your preference at any time in the Game's Settings menu under "Ad Preferences."

For all users: You can opt out of personalized advertising by:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Gameplay Data: For the lifetime of your account or until you request deletion
  • Device Identifiers: Until you reset your advertising ID or request deletion
  • Crash Logs: Up to 90 days
  • Analytics Data: Up to 26 months (anonymized)

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • Data is encrypted during transmission (TLS/SSL)
  • Local data is encrypted on your device
  • Cloud data is stored on secure Unity servers
  • Access to personal data is limited to authorized personnel

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of data processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below or use the "Delete My Data" option in the Game's Settings menu.

9. Children's Privacy

Battle of the Tribes is rated for ages 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information from our systems.
